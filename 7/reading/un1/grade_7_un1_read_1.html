🌟 Reading Passage

🌟 Unit 1: Appearance and Personality 🌟

🖱️ Altı çizili kelimenin Türkçe anlamını görmek için farenizi kelimenin üzerine getiriniz.

Hello! My name is Barry. Today, I want to introduce my two best classmates, Clara and Mark. Although they are both in my class, they have totally different physical appearances and personality traits.

Clara is a tall, slim girl with long wavy blonde hair and cheerful blue eyes. She is very outgoing and enjoys making new friends. She is also generous because she always shares her books and stationery with us. However, she can sometimes be a little forgetful; she occasionally leaves her homework at home!

On the other hand, Mark is shorter than Clara and has short curly dark hair. He wears glasses and looks quite smart. Mark is an introverted, quiet boy, but he is extremely punctual—he never arrives late for class. He is more hardworking than most of us. Sometimes Mark can be stubborn when working on group projects because he always wants to stick to his own plan. Even though Clara and Mark are so different, I love spending time with both of them!

Part 1: Multiple Choice Questions

1. What does Clara look like?
2. Why does Barry describe Clara as "generous"?
3. Which sentence is CORRECT when comparing Clara and Mark?
4. What is Mark's main negative personality trait mentioned in the text?
5. Which trait shows that Mark is "punctual"?

Part 2: Short Answer Questions

6. Write ONE physical appearance adjective used for Clara in the text.
7. What does Mark look like? (Mention hair type or accessories)
8. Which personality word in Paragraph 2 means "likely to forget things easily"?
9. Who is more hardworking according to Barry?
10. Write an adjective from the text that describes someone who never changes their opinion easily.
