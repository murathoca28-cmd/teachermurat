Comparing Friends: Alex and Liam

📖

Read the Text

Read about Alex and his best friend, Liam.

👆 Turkuaz renkli bir kelimeye dokun, Türkçe anlamını gör.

Hi! My name is Alex. Today, I want to tell you about my best friend, Liam. We are both in the 7th grade, but we have very different personalities and habits. I am 12 years old, so I am younger than Liam, who is 13. I am also taller than him, and I play basketball every weekend.

Liam has a lot of friends at school because he is more outgoing and more cheerful than me. He loves meeting new people and telling funny stories. He is also more talkative in class, while I am quiet and calm.

However, I am more punctual than Liam. I always arrive at school on time, but Liam is sometimes late. When it comes to school subjects, Liam is better than me at English, but my maths marks are higher than his. Even though we are different, we always enjoy spending time together!

✅

Part 1: Multiple Choice

Choose the correct answer.

1. Why does Liam have a lot of friends at school?
2. Who is younger according to the text?
3. Alex is more punctual than Liam because Alex ________.
4. In which school subject is Liam better than Alex?
5. Which sentence is CORRECT according to the text?
✏️

Part 2: Short Answers

Type a short answer.

6. Who is taller, Alex or Liam?
7. Write the comparative form of "good" used in the text to describe Liam's English skills.
8. Which boy is more talkative in class?
9. Which adjective in the text describes someone who always arrives on time?
10. How old is Liam?
