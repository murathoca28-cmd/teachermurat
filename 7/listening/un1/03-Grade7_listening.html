GRADE 7 LISTENING ACTIVITY 3

Meet Mia, Sarah & Leo!

Mia: Hi Leo! Have you met my new neighbor, Sarah?
Leo: Not yet. What is she like?
Mia: She is easy-going and thoughtful. She also wears glasses.
Leo: Cool! What about your brother? What does he look like?
Mia: He is well-built and tall.
Leo: And what is your brother like in daily life?
Mia: He is funny and outgoing.
Leo: Why do you like them so much?
Mia: I like them because they are kind and friendly.
Leo: That's great! What do you like doing together?
Mia: I like doing sports with them!

MULTIPLE CHOICE

1. What is Sarah like?

2. What does Mia's brother look like?

3. Why does Mia like her neighbor and brother?

SHORT ANSWER

4. Does Sarah wear glasses? (Yes / No)

5. Is Mia's brother tall or short?

6. What activity does Mia like doing with them?

Please answer all the questions before continuing.
