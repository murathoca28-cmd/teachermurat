My name is Elif. I am 13 years old. I am tall and slim, and I have long, straight, black hair and brown eyes. My best friend is Kaan. He is of medium height and well-built. He has short, curly, brown hair and green eyes. He wears glasses. Kaan is more outgoing than me because he loves meeting new people, but I am more punctual than him because I am never late.
