GRADE 5 LISTENING ACTIVITY 1

Preparing for Presentation

Transkript

Leo: Hi Maya, can you help me prepare for our presentation?
Maya: Sure! We need the projector to show our slides on the wall, right?
Leo: Yes. I printed these study sheets, but I need a stapler to join them.
Maya: Here is one. Where are the extra notes?
Leo: They are inside the orange folder.
Maya: Perfect. Take this yellow highlighter to mark the important words.
Leo: Thanks! Let me throw these useless papers into the rubbish bin, and we are ready.

MULTIPLE CHOICE

1. What do they need to show their slides on the wall?

2. What color is the folder?

3. Where does Leo throw the useless papers?

SHORT ANSWER

4. Who helps Leo prepare for the presentation?

5. What tool does Leo need to join the study sheets?

6. What is the yellow highlighter used for?
