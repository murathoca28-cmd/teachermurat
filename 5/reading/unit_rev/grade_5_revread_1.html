🌟 My Friends' Amazing Jobs 🌟

🖱️ Altı çizili kelimenin Türkçe anlamını görmek için farenizi kelimenin üzerine getiriniz.

Hello! My name is Alex. Today, I want to tell you about my family and their interesting jobs. My family is very hard-working!

My father is a police officer. He works at a big police station. He helps people and keeps our city safe. He wears a uniform every day. My mother is a doctor. She works at a hospital. She is very kind and she helps sick people. She sometimes works at night, but she loves her job!

My brother, Leo, is a farmer. He lives in a small village. He grows fresh fruits and vegetables. He also takes care of many animals. He gets up very early in the morning! My sister, Emma, is a singer. She is very talented. She sings songs in a studio with her band. She is very happy when she is on stage.

Finally, my uncle is a teacher. He works at a primary school. He teaches English to many students. He thinks his job is very important because he helps children learn new things. We are a very happy family!
