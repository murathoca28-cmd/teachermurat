🌟 Reading Passage

🌟 Lucy's Healthy Food Day 🌟

🖱️ Altı çizili kelimenin Türkçe anlamını görmek için farenizi kelimenin üzerine getiriniz.

Hello, I'm Lucy. Last weekend, I visited my grandmother. Every morning, she made a healthy breakfast for me. We ate eggs, yoghurt and spinach. My grandmother said, "Healthy food gives you energy!"

On Monday, my teacher, Mrs. Carter, brought a big poster to class. It had two columns: "Healthy Food" and "Unhealthy Food". She gave us some cards with pictures of food, like chips, broccoli, sweets and noodles. We had to put the cards in the right column. It was a fun game!

I put green salad, nuts and fish under "Healthy Food". I put hamburger, chocolate, sausages and noodles under "Unhealthy Food" too. My friend Daniel said, "Ice cream is healthy because it has milk!" But Mrs. Carter said, "No, ice cream has too much sugar. That is not good for our body."

That evening, I helped my mother pack my lunchbox for school. I chose fruit, green salad and water. I did not choose chips. My mother was proud of me. Now I eat more healthy food every day, and I feel happy and full of energy!

Part 1: Multiple Choice Questions

1. Where did Lucy go last weekend?
2. What did Lucy eat for breakfast?
3. What did Mrs. Carter bring to class?
4. Why is ice cream not healthy?
5. What did Lucy put in her lunchbox?

Part 2: Short Answer Questions

6. Who made breakfast for Lucy?
7. Write one healthy food from the passage.
8. Write one unhealthy food from the passage.
9. Who said ice cream was healthy?
10. How does Lucy feel now?
