What Makes a True Friend?

Transkript

What does friendship mean to you? For most people, a true friend is someone you can always count on. Friendship is built on trust, respect, and unconditional love. True friends share similar interests, support each other in tough times, and make life much happier.

But friendship isn't just about people! Many of us share a special bond with our pets, like dogs or cats. They may not speak our language, but they understand our feelings and offer pure loyalty.

As an old saying goes, 'When a person finds a true friend, he finds a priceless treasure.' So, cherish your friends, whether they have two legs or four!
