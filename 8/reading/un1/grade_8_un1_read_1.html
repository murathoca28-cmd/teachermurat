🌟 Reading Passage

🌟Unit 1: Friendship & Personal Traits🌟

🖱️ Altı çizili kelimenin Türkçe anlamını görmek için farenizi kelimenin üzerine getiriniz.

Friendship is not just about spending time together; it is about finding someone you can truly count on. My best friend Ege and I have known each other since primary school. Although we have different personalities—he is usually calm and laid-back, while I am more energetic—we get on well with each other. He is a tactful person who never hurts his friends' feelings.

True friends always back up each other when times are hard. Last Friday, Ege invited me to a rock concert. I had to refuse his offer at first because I needed to study for an important exam. When I explained my excuse, he didn't get angry; instead, he offered to study together after the event. A bad friend might be selfish or stubborn, but Ege always shows understanding.

We also have common hobbies like playing basketball and attending sci-fi film festivals. Having shared interests makes our bond even stronger. In short, a genuine companion should be reliable and supportive. I know that whenever I face a problem, I can rely on Ege without hesitation.
