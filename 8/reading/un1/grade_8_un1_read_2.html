🌟Friendship & Invitations 🌟

🖱️ Altı çizili kelimenin Türkçe anlamını görmek için farenizi kelimenin üzerine getiriniz.

Hi! My name is Alex. Friendship is very important to me because true buddies always back up each other. Yesterday, my best friend Liam sent me an invitation text message for his birthday party at a cafe this Saturday.

I really wanted to attend his party, but I had to refuse it. My volleyball team has an important match on the same day. I called Liam to state my excuse and apologize for missing his special day.

Liam was very understanding. He knows that I always count on him, and he supports my team, too. I made a promise to meet him next Sunday to celebrate his birthday together. We plan to go to the cinema and eat pizza. A good friend always keeps their word!

Part 1: Multiple Choice Questions

1. Why is Liam organizing a party?
2. Why does Alex refuse Liam's invitation?
3. How does Liam react to Alex's excuse?
4. What do Alex and Liam plan to do next Sunday?
5. Which statement is CORRECT according to the text?

Part 2: Short Answer Questions

6. Write the phrasal verb in the text that means "to support or help someone".
7. What sports match prevents Alex from attending the party?
8. When did Liam send the invitation text to Alex?
9. Where is Liam's birthday party taking place on Saturday?
10. Write the phrasal verb in the text that means "to trust or rely on someone".
